### 🎮 Why Boom Buds?

- **Up to 10 players** in a match, people and computer players mixed.
- **The whole couch on one device:** four players on one keyboard, two on each gamepad, touch controls on phones and tablets.
- **Online with zero setup:** games on your network show up by themselves, friends elsewhere join with a room code or a link. No server, no account.
- **Faithful rounds:** same arenas, rules and power-ups as the original, checked against it screenshot by screenshot.
- **Computer players worth beating,** from *Rookie* to *Master*, plus the original 1997 one.
- **On the TV too:** an Android TV and Google TV app, with gamepads and the remote.
